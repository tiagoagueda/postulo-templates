{% extends "documents/themes/base_calendar.html" %} {% comment %} The Calendar printed for a black-and-white printer (postulo/postulo#6): the month with every entry of each day, the week and the day as days one under another, and the agenda as the coming days with what is overdue. One template for the four views, which Postulo's base already tells apart. **Nothing is told apart by colour.** The kind of an entry is said in words, which Postulo writes before the title, and also drawn as the rule at the entry's start: solid, dashed, dotted, double, thick and thin. An entry that is over (a reminder done, an interview cancelled, a deadline met) is struck through, set in grey and marked with a cross, and the base says it in words too, so a page that comes out grey loses nothing. The week starts on the day the document's language's region starts it, and the weekday and month names are written by Postulo in the document's language: this adds no word of its own. A month wants landscape, which Postulo chooses for it; every measure here is a fraction of the page or a length that is the same on any paper. {% endcomment %} {% block page_styles %} body { font-family: "Helvetica Neue", Helvetica, Arial, "Liberation Sans", "Noto Sans", "Noto Sans Ethiopic", "Noto Naskh Arabic", sans-serif;
