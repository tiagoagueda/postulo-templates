{% load i18n %} {% comment %} The job-search report laid out the way France Travail's adviser reads a jobseeker's steps (postulo/postulo#1262): the applications of the period, then the interviews, in the two groups France Travail's own tool for recording steps ("l'organisation des démarches") uses. In France the proof of a search is the copies of the applications and the employers' replies (service-public.gouv.fr, fiche F14926), so this page is a summary of them to type from, to bring to an appointment or to send with the copies; it says so, and it says it is the person's own record and not a France Travail form. No mark, no logo, nothing drawn to look like an official form. Drawn whole like `handover`, not over `base_report.html`, which opens with the figures. Plain values only. What Postulo does not hold (the France Travail identifier) is a blank line for the pen; no contact's or interviewer's name is ever printed, and no target number of applications: that is the person's and the adviser's, not the document's. The core that names how each application was made and lists the period's interviews one by one (postulo/postulo#1262) hands `interviews` as a list, empty or not; an older core does not hand it at all. `interviews is not None` tells the two apart, so this page draws on either: without them, the column and the second table are left out rather than empty.
